FAQ

Questions, Answered

What do I need to start?
A phone number that is not already on the WhatsApp app, and a free Meta Business account. Our step-by-step guide connects it to the official WhatsApp Cloud API.
How are messages charged?
You pay a fixed plan fee plus a per-message charge from a prepaid wallet (Marketing, Utility or Authentication). Replies to customers within 24 hours of their message are free.
Is my number safe from bans?
We use only Meta’s official API, approved templates and automatic opt-out (customers can reply STOP). Message only customers who agreed to hear from you.
